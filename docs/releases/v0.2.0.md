The first release of DrillBalancer: the firmware for the Waveshare ESP32-S3
AMOLED 1.75 board and the Android app.

## Firmware

- One level screen instead of three modes. The device shows the bubble
  (LEVEL) when it lies flat or the bit points at the floor or the ceiling,
  and the crosshair (WALL) when the bit points at a wall. It switches by
  itself.
- The bubble reads the device's own face, so the device lying flat on a
  surface works as a plain level.
- Zero works on the wall view: hold the screen for 1 s. The zero stays
  while you lay the drill down and pick it up again.
- The angle mode, the target angle and the mode swipe are gone.
- The level dot moves smoothly again, also while recording over Wi-Fi.
- BOOT opens the settings.
- New setting: Invert dot.
- Training data: records the motion sensor and both microphones over the
  device's own Wi-Fi.

## App

- The live view shows Wall or Level, as the device does. Zero is greyed out
  on the level view.
- The settings no longer have Mode and Target.
- Training data: record takes, label them automatically, review them and
  upload them to your data repository.
- Offers new firmware and app versions from GitHub, with these notes under
  What's new.

## Update order

Install this app first, then update the firmware from it. An older app
can't connect to this firmware.
